# Komplyint Oy — Sales, freelance-project qualification and delivery playbook

**Date:** 2026-10-29  
**Scope:** Partner subcontracting, freelance specialist placement and direct B2B delivery.  
**Status:** Sales guidance/operating standard. No outreach or contract has been sent/accepted by this documentation commit.

## Strategic goal

Pursue suitable contracts **actively and systematically** using:
1. A Finnish-and-English-speaking lead consultant for data, BI, QA, operational reporting and Jira/project tracking.
2. An **available-by-agreement English-speaking full-stack engineer** (Valentine Idi, 5+ years' professional evidence) with TypeScript/JavaScript, React, Angular, Vue, Node.js; further C#/.NET, Electron, REST API, Firebase, Kafka and Azure Functions experience evidenced in his CV.
3. Joint delivery, if a customer accepts Finnish-language communication through the lead and English-language technical implementation.
4. Other vetted specialists, recruited/contracted **when** the engagement requires them and after confirming buyer approval.
5. AI-assisted internal workflows when technically sound and contractually/data-protection compliant.

**Do not infer the user personally has the brother's stack.** The stack note “My Stack is typescript/javascript, node.js (backend), angular, vue and react (frontend)” was shared as a note accompanying Valentine's CV.

**Sales stance:** Lead with relevant value, not a list of every skill; always invite other requirements. Never say “we can do anything” or promise undemonstrated proficiency. Do not present speculative work as current project references.

## Target buyers (priority order)

### Track A — specialist placements, faster validation
- Finnish freelance/consulting intermediaries and IT delivery partners with **active English-speaking** React / Angular / Vue / TypeScript / JavaScript / Node.js / API integration / full-stack assignments.
- Parallel track for **Finnish/English** Power BI, data quality, QA, Jira/project-coordination, operational reporting roles for the owner.
- Prioritise ads that specify a workable start date, permitted remote/hybrid location, language fit, stack fit, commercial rate and company invoicing.
- A Scrum Master role **requiring Finnish and actual Scrum facilitation** is not a fit for Valentine solely because he writes software in agile teams. Only apply if the buyer explicitly accepts the delivery person's language and evidence.

### Track B — direct project buyers, shorter sales cycle at smaller firms
- Companies needing custom internal applications, integrations, web feature development, e-commerce operations, reporting and automated office workflows.
- Split into landing-page features, front-end rewrites, backend/API jobs, dashboard/API integrations, QA regression, business process automation.
- Address tangible workflow and user needs; qualify existing supplier arrangements, urgency and decision authority.

### Track C — larger enterprises
- Potential long-term vendor frameworks, departmental projects and specialised engineering capacity.
- Expect vendor onboarding, data/privacy documentation, references, procurement requirements and longer sales cycles; do not treat turnover alone as strong conversion evidence.

## Opportunity qualification: one-row evidence per lead

Required fields:
- Buyer/company and relationship type (direct / broker / partner), source URL, listing date and status.
- Project purpose, outputs, tech stack, seniority, duration, **actual required allocation**, budget/rate if publicly specified, invoice terms.
- Geography/onsite/remote/working hours and **required working language of each role** (not just contact language).
- Named consultant eligibility, verified relevant experience, location, client acceptance of English technical work, NDA/security clearance/right to work and subcontracting constraints.
- Evidence of a current need (open role/explicit partner programme vs inferred from sector).
- Next action and approval deadline; sent-contact log, response status and opt-out/suppression state.
- Skills gap and staffing/AI feasibility path.

Classify: **A immediate fit; B potential after client clarification; C opportunity mismatched or speculative**.
Do not automatically contact all ads: prioritise role fit and legitimate buyer channels.

## First-contact formula

1. Refer to **one actual buyer need** or a credible partnership link.
2. Briefly identify Komplyint Oy and the **most relevant** capability (one or two, not seven).
3. Where relevant mention that other verified development/data/QA specialists can be proposed and that expert resource allocation or scoped delivery can be discussed.
4. Ask what the buyer's **current and forthcoming** projects require — this leaves room for alternative roles and prevents an over-narrow answer.
5. Keep the email moderate, direct, professional and natural in Finnish/English; do not pitch as a large staffing firm.
6. End the draft at **“Ystävällisin terveisin,”** (FI), **“Kind regards,”** (EN), or **“Med vänliga hälsningar,”** (SV). **NEVER append name, Komplyint Oy, address, URL or any other signature:** the owner's Zoho client automatically inserts a complete signature.
7. Keep To and From verified in Zoho. An offline HTML mailto link cannot enforce the sender; use the configured business mailbox.
8. Manual, reviewed individual email outreach; no unsolicited bulk mail blasts. Follow the prospect's public application/supplier channel and suppress opt-outs.

## When an incoming response declines a narrow offer

- Thank them, acknowledge the specific rejection.
- Explain only if useful that the first email intentionally proposed **one concrete potential collaboration** rather than listing a whole portfolio.
- If there is no “do not contact” instruction, a single concise note may mention wider capabilities and an open invitation. **Then stop unless they respond**.
- Record explicit opt-out immediately.
- Avoid implying the customer has poor testing processes or missing skills.

## Consultant submission and client contract rules

- For specific roles: one sanitized **named professional CV**, explicit location and **individual working language**, concrete experiences, exact available allocation. A company profile alone rarely substitutes for a consultant CV.
- For projects: company credentials, proposed solution and milestones, named responsible person, acceptance criteria, IP/licensing, warranty, invoicing, support terms and change requests.
- Brokering/adding subcontractors requires **documented customer agreement** where required; never silently replace a named person. If a client restricts AI tooling, outside service providers or customer-data transfer, comply before work.
- CVs/reference contacts, personal email/phone, customer secrets and confidential project materials must stay outside public GitHub and unsolicited emails.
- If an opportunity requests security clearance, certification, fluent Finnish or a minimum number of years for a particular skill, verify for **the proposed individual**, not the company in the abstract.

## Delivery safeguards for AI-assisted work

Practical opportunities: code suggestions with developer review, API scaffolding, unit/test cases, data-cleaning scripts, report explanations, internal knowledge search, approved document classification.

Before starting: scope, acceptance tests, data access, customer-approved tool use, retention/residency, IP ownership, accessibility/performance, documented deployment and named human reviewer. **No client-confidential data to unapproved external tools.** AI-generated code must be code-reviewed, tested, and licensed/secured appropriately.

No unsupported promise of “any project”, “AI will do it”, automatic guaranteed delivery of unstaffed specialties or 24/7 monitoring.

## Outreach and follow-up workflow

- Research leads and deduplicate against all companies already contacted, including brand/group overlaps.
- Prioritise official partner application routes and role-specific contacts. Named personal work inboxes require role relevance and applicable consent/legitimate B2B marketing checks; do not invent email addresses.
- For every contact save verification date, source URL, fit, message, send date, response, follow-up status, suppression/opt-out and next action.
- Send only after human approval and true supplier fit; do not submit applications by email when the listing requires portal-only applications.
- Run a short pipeline review weekly to identify replies and projects; avoid sending repeat pitches after a refusal.
- For live advertised roles, act quickly with an appropriate, confirmed specialist profile — **do not wait to build a full portfolio before responding**, but do not exaggerate skills.

## Existing prospect lessons

- **Taskmill:** Buyer asked about expert placements at 80–100% allocation over six months to two years. Useful direction for named consultants; never conclude a specific vacancy was offered.
- **Finitec:** Referred Komplyint to their live gigs; roles vary by duration, seniority, work language. English-speaking software consultant may fit some projects; a Finnish-mandatory Scrum Master role should be screened out unless individual requirements change.
- **Exove:** Responded that internal QA/test processes cover their needs. Respect that rejection. A wider one-time introduction (if not a do-not-contact) can mention software/data/automation; no repeat pressure.
- **JT AI Labs:** Example of individual-expert freelance evaluation; confirm whether corporate invoicing/substitution is allowed. Do not imply all person-specific assignments can be subcontracted.
