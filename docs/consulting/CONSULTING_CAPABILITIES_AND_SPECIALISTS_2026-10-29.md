## 5. Proposed B2B business model

The company can offer, **subject to assignment acceptance and specialist availability**:
1. **Individual expert contracting / staff augmentation:** part-time or 80–100% allocation for multi-month assignments; the named consultant, working language, location/hybrid rules, seniority and right to subcontract must match the customer's requirements.
2. **Scoped fixed-price delivery:** defined web application feature, API, report, test suite, integration, automation or data cleanup, with acceptance criteria, delivery artefacts, and an agreed change process.
3. **Combined specialist delivery:** Finnish-speaking requirements/coordination and English-speaking software implementation where the buyer approves this model.
4. **Additional staffing:** propose vetted employees/subcontractors when project size or expertise demands it; confirm written approval and associated privacy/security terms.
5. **Maintenance/retainer:** optional agreed release support, monitoring and improvements; do **not** imply 24/7 support or a team on standby.
